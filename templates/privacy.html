Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Spendly collects, why we need it and what control you have over it.

1. What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

  • Your name, so we can greet you and personalise your account.
  • Your email address, which you use to sign in.
  • Your password, which is stored hashed. We never store or see it in plain text.
  • Your expense entries, such as amount, category, date and description.

2. Why we collect it

We use this information only to create and secure your account, save your expenses and show you summaries of your spending. We don't use it for advertising.

3. How it's stored

Your data is stored in a local SQLite database on the server that runs Spendly. Passwords are hashed before they are saved.

4. No selling or sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties.

5. Cookies and sessions

Spendly uses a single session cookie to keep you signed in. We don't use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your personal details and expense entries are removed from the database.

7. Your rights

You're in control of your data. You can:

  • View your profile and all of your expenses.
  • Edit your details or any expense entry.
  • Delete individual expenses, or ask us to delete your whole account.

8. Children's privacy

Spendly is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date above. See also our Terms & Conditions.
